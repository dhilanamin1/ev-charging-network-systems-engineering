# Stakeholder Needs
### EV Charging Network — Inglewood Civic Center

_Identifies the system's stakeholders and what each one needs from the station. Each is a given an ID number for traceability in further deliverables_

## Needs
| ID | Stakeholder | Need |
|---|---|---|
| N-1 | Driver | Drivers want available, working chargers and need to know whether a charger is available at the station. |
| N-2 | Driver | Drivers want short wait times for charging during busy periods. |
| N-3 | Driver | Drivers want to charge within a reasonable time and get enough charge to go about their daily activities. |
| N-4 | Driver | Drivers want accessible, easy payment options for a stress-free transaction. |
| N-5 | Station Operator | Operators need easy access to charger status, utilization, and wait times, especially during busy periods. |
| N-7 | Station Operator | During failures, operators need to see exactly what is mechanically or electrically at fault. |
| N-8 | City of Inglewood | The City wants easy public accessibility to the chargers. |
| N-9 | City of Inglewood | The City wants construction and operations to stay within budget. |
| N-10 | City of Inglewood | The City needs the station to comply with city ordinances and safety regulations. |
| N-11 | Maintenance / Cleaning Staff | Staff need to know which charger is broken and understand what is broken and why. |
| N-12 | Electrical Grid Provider | The grid provider needs to know the station's total power draw and needs the station to stay within its allocated supply. |
| N-13 | Payment Processing Operator | Wants simple, smooth payment processing while ensuring the security of payments. |
| N-14 | City of Inglewood | The City wants the remaining chargers to stay accessible and working when one or more chargers fail. |
