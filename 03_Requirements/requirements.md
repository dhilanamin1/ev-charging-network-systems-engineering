# System Requirements
### EV Charging Network — Inglewood Civic Center

_Defines the system requirements for the station. Each requirement uses "shall" to indicate a binding obligation, is written to be verifiable, and traces to the stakeholder need (N-#) it satisfies. Requirements are typed as Functional (a capability the system provides) or Performance (a measurable level of performance)._

_**Note on performance targets:** Performance numbers (e.g., 90%, 250 kW, 60 seconds) are initial design targets to be later refined through trade study and simulation phases._

_**Note on N-9 (budget):** The budget need is addressed as a project constraint (see ConOps, Section 6) rather than a system requirement, as it governs the project rather than system behavior._

## Requirements

| ID | Type | Requirement | Traces To |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-1 | Functional | The system shall display each charger's status (available or unavailable) on the station screen. | N-1 |
| R-2 | Performance | The system shall provide charging to at least 90% of vehicles arriving during peak hours without turning them away. | N-2 |
| R-3 | Performance | The system shall provide a minimum power output of 250 kW for each charging unit. | N-3 |
| R-4 | Functional | The system shall provide station operators the ability to view each charger's status (available or unavailable). | N-5 |
| R-5 | Functional | The system shall provide station operators the ability to view charger wait times during busy periods. | N-5 |
| R-6 | Functional | The system shall provide station operators the ability to view mechanical or electrical faults and errors. | N-7 |
| R-7 | Performance | The system shall complete payment processing within 60 seconds or less. | N-4 |
| R-8 | Functional | The system shall accept multiple payment methods, including credit and debit cards. | N-4 |
| R-9 | Functional | The system shall provide at least one ADA-accessible charging unit. | N-8 |
| R-10 | Functional | The system shall meet applicable electrical and safety standards and City of Inglewood ordinances. | N-10 |
| R-11 | Functional | The system shall provide maintenance and cleaning staff the ability to view why a charger is non-operational or operating below full capacity. | N-11 |
| R-12 | Functional | The system shall provide the ability to view the total power output of the station. | N-12 |
| R-13 | Performance | The system shall keep total power drawn within available electrical grid capacity. | N-12 |
| R-14 | Functional | The system shall provide secure payment processing that adheres to payment-security standards. | N-13 |
| R-15 | Functional | The system shall continue providing access to other functional chargers while a charger is unavailable or operating below full capacity. | N-14 |
